Make mobile mapping data ready for asset workflows.

GeoProcess Consulting supports downstream processing of mobile-mapping datasets supplied by clients. Depending on the available inputs and specification, work may include point-cloud organization, classification, feature extraction, mapping layers and QA/QC for corridor or asset programs.

OUR APPROACH

A structured workflow from source data to usable deliverables.

Mobile mapping may combine dense point clouds, imagery and positioning information. Processing should preserve the source coordinate reference, trajectory context and required feature attributes while preparing consistent outputs for the next system.

01 · REVIEW

Inputs and reference systems

Inspect dataset completeness, coordinate metadata, trajectory or image references, file segmentation and the requested output schema.

02 · PROCESS

Classification and feature extraction

Organize point-cloud data and extract required assets or mapped features according to the project-defined classes and attributes.

03 · DELIVER

Corridor and asset-ready data

Prepare structured outputs, review spatial alignment, check feature completeness and document exceptions or data gaps.

CORRIDOR AND STREET-LEVEL DATA

Turn moving-platform scans into usable inventory.

Mobile mapping data can combine LiDAR, imagery and a positioning trajectory. The processing workflow must consider sensor alignment, trajectory quality, occlusion and how features will be consumed by GIS, CAD or asset teams.

01 / ALIGN

Trajectory and point-cloud review

Check coordinate reference, trajectory coverage, strip consistency and obvious offsets. GNSS/IMU quality and synchronization influence how well the point cloud fits mapped features.

02 / EXTRACT

Road and roadside assets

Prepare requested classes or features such as road edges, signs, poles, kerbs, façades, vegetation, street furniture and selected corridor assets when the source data supports them.

03 / HAND OVER

GIS, CAD and QA outputs

Deliver organized point clouds, vector layers, inventory attributes and exception notes in formats and schemas agreed for the client's asset system.

Scope depends on visibility:

Features behind vehicles, inside dense vegetation or outside the sensor field of view may be incomplete. We identify coverage gaps rather than imply every asset is present in the source scan.
