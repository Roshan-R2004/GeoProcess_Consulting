OUR APPROACH

A structured workflow from source data to usable deliverables.

Scan-to-BIM deliverables require a clear specification for coordinate systems, registration quality, modeling rules, tolerances and information requirements. Defining these at the start reduces ambiguity during production and review.

01 · DEFINE

LOD, use and tolerances

Agree target use, LOD/LOI, required disciplines, modeling tolerances, naming, coordinate system and exchange formats.

02 · MODEL

Point-cloud-driven geometry

Develop the agreed building or asset elements from the supplied scan data, using the selected BIM workflow and model standards.

03 · VERIFY

As-built review and handover

Review fit to the available reference data, element completeness, information fields and model organization before delivery.

SCAN-TO-BIM DELIVERY

Build the model around its intended use.

Scan-to-BIM transforms client-provided point clouds, scans and references into structured building information models. The right model scope depends on the purpose, required categories, level of development and tolerance agreed with the client.

01 / DEFINE

LOD, scope and tolerances

Agree the elements to model, required parameters, coordinate setup, exclusions, modelling tolerance and use case before production.

02 / MODEL

Architecture, structure and MEP

Build the required Revit/BIM elements from the available scan evidence. Complex geometry, occluded areas and incomplete scan coverage may need assumptions or client decisions.

03 / QA

Alignment and model review

Check model-to-point-cloud fit, levels, coordinates, categories, family behavior, naming and model usability for the agreed downstream workflow.

Important:

LOD is not a universal accuracy promise. The level of detail, information content and geometric tolerance should be documented separately for each project.
