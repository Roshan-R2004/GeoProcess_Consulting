Before you reach out

Questions clients usually ask.

A few practical answers before you send the first message.

What should I send first?

Send the dataset type, approximate project size, coordinate system if known, desired output and target timeline. If some of these are unknown, that is completely fine.

Can you review a sample before a full production scope?

Yes. A representative sample is often the best way to confirm classification expectations, output structure and quality requirements before scaling production.

Do you work with client-supplied survey data?

Yes. GeoProcess is positioned on the processing side of the workflow: we can work from supplied point clouds, imagery and other spatial datasets and prepare structured downstream outputs.

Can we discuss the project before everything is finalized?

Absolutely. Use the booking page if you would prefer a structured conversation rather than preparing a complete written brief first.
