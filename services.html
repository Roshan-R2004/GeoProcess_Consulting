Core capabilities

Geospatial production from raw data to engineering-ready delivery.

Our services cover the full processing chain across LiDAR, GIS, imagery, point clouds, terrain, CAD, BIM, utilities and spatial analytics. Each discipline can stand alone or connect into a complete production workflow.

01 · LIDAR

LiDAR Data Processing & Classification

Convert raw point clouds into accurate, structured classes and engineering-ready information.

  • Ground, non-ground & vegetation classification
  • Noise removal, cleansing & QA/QC
  • Feature extraction, vectorization & point-cloud colorization
02 · GIS

GIS Mapping & Spatial Data Management

Build reliable spatial databases, maps and structured layers for analysis and asset management.

  • GIS data capture, conversion & structuring
  • Parcel, utility & infrastructure mapping
  • Spatial analysis, map production & GIS databases
03 · 3D / MODELING

3D Mapping & Geospatial Modeling

Create measurable 3D representations of terrain, corridors, assets, structures and project environments.

  • 3D asset and corridor modeling
  • Terrain and infrastructure visualization
  • Models for GIS, CAD and engineering workflows
04 · LASER SCANNING

3D Laser Scanning & Scan-to-BIM

Turn terrestrial laser-scanning and point-cloud data into accurate as-built models for buildings, structures and MEP.

  • Point-cloud based as-built documentation
  • Architectural, structural & MEP modeling
  • Scan-to-BIM and facility-ready outputs
05 · PHOTOGRAMMETRY

Photogrammetry & Aerial Mapping

Derive measurements, maps and 3D models from overlapping aerial and terrestrial imagery.

  • Ground control and tie-point workflows
  • Block adjustment and 3D reconstruction
  • Topographic and mapping outputs
06 · ORTHOPHOTO

Orthophoto & Ortho-Mosaic Production

Generate high-precision orthophotos and ortho-mosaics from aerial and stereo satellite imagery.

  • Aerial triangulation
  • DEM, DTM & contour generation
  • Planimetric and topographic mapping
07 · TERRAIN

DTM, DSM & DEM Generation

Produce dependable terrain and surface models from LiDAR, imagery and elevation datasets.

  • Bare-earth DTM
  • DSM surface models
  • Contours, elevation products & terrain analysis
08 · BIM

BIM Services — LOD 400 & LOD 500

Develop structural and MEP BIM models from coordination-level work through fabrication and facility-management outputs.

  • Structural & MEP BIM modeling
  • LOD 400 fabrication-oriented models
  • LOD 500 data-rich facility models
09 · MOBILE MAPPING

Mobile Mapping & Asset Extraction

Process geo-referenced 360° imagery and mobile mapping data for roads, utilities, public assets and right-of-way inventories.

  • Road, sidewalk and vegetation assets
  • Utility and right-of-way inventories
  • Condition and location attributes for GIS
10 · UTILITIES

Utility, Pipeline & Telecom GIS

Prepare spatial datasets and feature extraction workflows for network, corridor and infrastructure programs.

  • Electric, gas, sewer & storm networks
  • Pipeline and corridor feature extraction
  • Telecom GIS and asset mapping
11 · CAD / ENGINEERING

CAD Conversion & Engineering Data

Transform survey, scan and spatial information into clean CAD-ready drawings and structured engineering deliverables.

  • GIS-to-CAD and CAD conversion
  • Asset and feature extraction
  • Engineering-ready drawing outputs
12 · ANALYSIS / QA

Geospatial Automation, Analysis & QA/QC

Build repeatable workflows and quality checks that improve consistency across large geospatial production programs.

  • Spatial analysis and decision-ready outputs
  • Production QA/QC and validation
  • Data annotation and repeatable automation workflows
How the services connect

One dataset can support an entire project.

We approach every assignment as a controlled information pipeline. The exact steps vary by project, but the principle remains the same: preserve spatial integrity while progressively increasing usefulness.

01 · RECEIVE

Project Data

LiDAR, imagery, point clouds, specifications and existing spatial information.

02 · PREPARE

Normalize & Inspect

Coordinate systems, data integrity, tiles, density and project requirements.

03 · PROCESS

Classify & Extract

Ground, vegetation, structures, features and terrain are organized.

04 · ANALYZE

Model & Understand

Surfaces, corridors, assets and spatial relationships become measurable.

05 · DELIVER

Engineering-Ready

GIS, CAD, BIM, 3D, reports and structured datasets ready for use.

Flagship capability · Utility intelligence

Powerline vegetation danger-zone analytics.

For utility corridors, the point is not simply to see vegetation. It is to understand its relationship with the line, structure, terrain and clearance envelope—and turn that relationship into actionable information.
