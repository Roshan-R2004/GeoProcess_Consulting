How the services connect

One dataset can support an entire project.

We approach every assignment as a controlled information pipeline. The exact steps vary by project, but the principle remains the same: preserve spatial integrity while progressively increasing usefulness.

01 · RECEIVE

Project Data

LiDAR, imagery, point clouds, specifications and existing spatial information.

02 · PREPARE

Normalize & Inspect

Coordinate systems, data integrity, tiles, density and project requirements.

03 · PROCESS

Classify & Extract

Ground, vegetation, structures, features and terrain are organized.

04 · ANALYZE

Model & Understand

Surfaces, corridors, assets and spatial relationships become measurable.

05 · DELIVER

Engineering-Ready

GIS, CAD, BIM, 3D, reports and structured datasets ready for use.

Flagship capability · Utility intelligence

Powerline vegetation danger-zone analytics.

For utility corridors, the point is not simply to see vegetation. It is to understand its relationship with the line, structure, terrain and clearance envelope—and turn that relationship into actionable information.
